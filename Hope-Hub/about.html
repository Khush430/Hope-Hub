Our story

Compassion in action, every single day

We are Yajna for Humanity — an organisation committed to positively impacting the lives of those in need. Founded on a vision of compassion and a mission of service, we work to address critical social challenges while assisting vulnerable people and communities.

With a strong commitment to charity, we focus our efforts on a variety of causes: disaster relief, humanitarian aid, healthcare access, animal welfare and environmental conservation. Through strategic collaborations we aim to establish long-term solutions that inspire change and improve wellbeing — all through one unified online platform.

Our values

Four principles shape every project we run and every pound we spend.

Compassion

We lead with empathy, treating every person and animal we help with dignity and respect.

Transparency

Donors can see exactly where their contributions go and the difference they make.

Community

We partner with local people, because lasting change is built from within communities.

Sustainability

We invest in long-term programmes and conservation, not just short-term relief.
