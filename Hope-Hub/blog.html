Featured story

A year of crisis — and of extraordinary generosity

The last 12 months have been some of the hardest we've ever seen. Families, communities and livelihoods have been destroyed by frequent and deadly extreme weather events — earthquakes, heatwaves, fires, floods and storms — and by ongoing and escalating conflict.

Yet through it all, our donors stepped up. Thanks to your support, our rapid-response teams reached 28 communities this year with shelter, food and medical care.
